Move Daily

Your daily movement habit, built in minutes.

Move Daily is a personal wellness app for iPhone that helps you build short, consistent workout sessions — stretching, breathing, bodyweight, and balance exercises — with voice-guided coaching. Choose a session length that fits your day, follow the guided cues, and watch your streak grow. No accounts, no internet required, and no data ever leaves your device.
